Privacy policy — Neural Ambient Behavior Unit (NABU-7X)

Developer: Iron Beaver Works
Contact: ironbeaverworks@gmail.com
Last updated: November 10, 2026

Neural Ambient Behavior Unit is an Android live wallpaper and interactive HUD. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, or Internet permission. It does not upload your photos, location, settings, or sensor readings, or share them with the developer or third parties.

Camera and photos

With your permission and an explicit button action, the app takes one front-camera photo after a two-second visual effect. There is no camera preview or audio recording. Android's camera privacy indicator remains visible. Photos are saved to Pictures/Cyberdeck in your device's media library. The app does not perform facial recognition or biometric identification. Camera permission is also used for the optional rear flashlight.

Location

Optional approximate or precise location positions the globe marker and displays coordinates. The app uses Android location providers and may read their last known fix. Location updates stop when the owning HUD screen or wallpaper engine is hidden. The app does not request background-location permission or keep a saved location history. Android's own location services are subject to your device settings and their providers' policies.

Device and sensor readings

The HUD reads available battery, charging, temperature, pressure, ambient light, magnetic field, orientation, memory, storage capacity, and mobile/Wi-Fi connection state or signal readings. These values are processed on the device. Short instrument histories are held in process memory for up to ten minutes and disappear when the process ends. It does not read the contents of your files or record microphone audio.

Settings and retention

Appearance, arrangement, calibration, and disclosure preferences are stored in the app's private local settings until changed, cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Photos remain in the device media library until you delete them, including after uninstalling the app. Your gallery or device backup services may independently synchronize photos according to your settings; the app does not control those services. The app disables its own cloud backup and excludes app data from Android 12+ device-transfer rules.

Your choices and deletion

Camera and location are optional. You can deny or revoke permissions in Android Settings and continue using the remaining features. Delete saved photos using your gallery or file manager, and clear the app's storage in Android Settings to remove its preferences. No developer-side account or data store exists to delete.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Iron Beaver Works at ironbeaverworks@gmail.com. This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
